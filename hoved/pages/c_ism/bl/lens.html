Nastik Jibondrishti

Protifolonbad, Ostitbobad ebong Nirothoktobad

Nastikota Bisshash Mota Mot
Shongkhipto Bishleshon:

Mull Adhunik Kothamara Guli

Sob jibondritter drishtibhongi sheshe phire ek shokti bihin byabosthar duniya ke bujhar jonno tin-ti mull adhunik kothamara er upor depend kore. Setar kono fokir, grohoshtho, bidrohi, ba noishthik byabashayi hote chaile kono farak pore na. Jokhon divya prodanota, bishsho udhesho, ebong abritto darsota shoriye phelo, setarpor tin-ti chap abar phire ashe. Tomar kache prodanotar chara bhabna kora dorkar. Tomar kache bishsho udheshor chara banchite dorkar. Tomar kache bishsho nijei bujhte akkhkhushi thakleo onushtito thakar ekta poth dorkar.

Ei chap guli tin-ti alhada poth toiri kore. Reflectionism toettho shambodhona kore. Existentialism udhesho shambodhona kore. Absurdism onushthan shambodhona kore. Tara nostikotar chintar mull bhitti toiri kore karon tara tin-ti shomosha shoman kore je kokhono chole na. Jekono nostikotar upor bhitti toiri kora dritti sheshe ei tin-tir angsho abar toiri kore, joto-tuku setar bhinno naam ba norom bhasha byabohar kora hoy.

Reflectionism bhitti toiri kore dabi kore je prodhanoto earn korte hobe, prodanotar chara. Existentialism ghotona toiri kore manusher byabachana theke, bishsho prodhanotar chara. Absurdism ghotona onushthito rakhe bishsho nijer noshto shikar kore, kono byakhya toiri korar chara.

Ei sob milke, kalponik chara, abritto shahojjo chara, ebong bishsho prodhanotar guarantee chara jibon kharap korte ekta poth toiri kore. Nostikota kalponike toettho mone kore dhora theke mukti kore. Jokhon eta chap chariye jay, manusher kache ekta shohoj proshno thake: Script chara kivabe banchben. Ei tin-ti pradhon jibondritter drishtibhongi nostikotar bhitti theke swabhabik vabe grow kore. Tara protisphurdha kore na. Tara bhinno poth byabashayin vabe byakhya kore je bishsho ready-made artho distribute korte rajji na. Pratiti alhada vabe uncertainty shambodhona kore. Pratiti alhada vabe swatantrata shambodhona kore. Ebong pratiti alhada vabe swatantrata chara, prodanotar theke golpo dhariye nijer jibon kharap karte ekta poth deye.

Toettho:

Reflectionism: Sobcheye Beshi Chintamoyon

Reflectionism ekta shohoj niti diye shuru kore. Jodi ekta dabi khamota chay, tahole setake earn korte hobe. Reflectionism protiti bhabnake ekta atithi hishebe dekhe. Seta pravesh korte pare. Seta kotha bolte pare. Kintu seta thakte pare na jodi seta nijer proshiddhi toiri na kore. Ei vababei kalponike onushthito rakhe. Eta manusher ke golpo enjoy korte deye jokhon tara narokore. Eta manusher ke golpo theke shikkha nite deye jokhon tara golpo ke toettho mone kore na.

Reclassification Reflectionism er gothonamulluk shakha. Eta reflectionism er earn kora prodhanoto demand niye protishthaner upor apply kore. Jodi kono bhabna automatic bishwas pete na, tahole kono dhormo automatic ainor shurakhsha pele na. Ekjon reflectionism dekhe kivabe shomaj dhormiyon shreni ke uccho kore ebong birodh dekhe. Dabiguli bishesh proshiddhi earn kore nai, kintu protishthan guli take mone kore jeno seta earn korechilo. Reclassification Nastikota ei mismatch fix kore. Eta soman odhikar, soman tolosho, ebong soman daittobolitwar pomot kore. Ar kichhu na. Ar kom o na.

Antitheism Reflectionism er noitik shakha. Eta tokhon dekha jay jokhon earn na kora prodhanota khotir karon hoy. Ekjon reflectionism mulladhikar dhormoke akromon kore na. Seta dhormiyo byabostha ke akromon kore je guli prodhan pramaner upor bhitti kore na, shongshoy ke shashon kore, ba manushder swatantrata simito kore. Antitheism Reflectionism je tokhon noshto hoy jokhon foliter mukhe noshto thakte rajji na. Eta nijer jonno odhikombol na. Eta byabosthar prati pratyashortor folita, je guli prodhaner chay, pramaner na.

Ei duti shakha milke dekhay kivabe Reflectionism grow kore. Eta mon theke shuru hoy. Ainer dike expand kore. Noitiker dike pravesh kore. Ekta niti, tin-ti jagar prodhan. Prodhanoto jekhanei thakuk, setake earn korte hobe.

Reflectionism thanda shongshoy noy. Eta sakriyo chinta. Eta assumptions shobcheye pora chinta kora, rakhano na. Eta kalponike prakritik toettho ke byabashayin korte manjuri na. Reflectionism manusher ke proshno kore je artho experience theke toiri koro, abritti theke na. Eta shongshoy ke ekta tool mone kore, bhoy noy. Ekjon reflectionism ekta kalponik nayoker prashansa korte pare, tar theke shikkha nite pare, ebong chole jete pare. Tara kalponik enjoy korte pare jokhon tar upor rajjota na kore. Tara inspire nite pare order chara. Reflectionism mon ke shafol rakhe kalponik theke amader je kichhu move kore, sei theke je kichhu amader control kore tike alhada kore. Ei drishtibhongi manushder jonno je guli karon karon noy, artho myth chara, ebong shohoj onushthan comfort-er beshi.

Udesho:

Existentialism: Byabachana ebong Daittobolita

Existentialism Reflectionism jekhane fele dey shekhane shuru hoy. Jokhon jaboriyo kalponik shoriye gelo, manusher kache ekti fankha jayga thake. Script noy. Bishsho prodhan noy. Purbo-nirdishto udhesho noy. Existentialism bole je ei fankha jayga swatantrata. Artho khujja hoy na. Seta toiri hoy. Manusher byabachana kore tar mull. Tara byabachana kore tar daittobolita. Tara byabachana kore je dharar jibon tara toiri korte chan. Ei byabachana take beshi define kore joto abritto golpo kore na. Eta drishtibhongi manushder jonno je guli agency chan, nijer poth ghiren chan, ebong artho ke arekjon er golpo theke nite rajji na.

Existentialism kothin. Eta manusher ke nijeder kormo janno dayitto nite encourage kore, noi bikkhov ba onushthan ke doshi kore. Eta manusher ke byabostha theke identity build korte encourage kore, noi parampara theke. Eta manusher ke swikar korte encourage kore je swatantrata er shongne weight ache. Kintu Existentialism shoktishali-o. Eta manusher ke nijer jiboner ownership deye. Eta manusher ke abar lekhte deye. Eta manusher ke artho toiri korte deye je tar reality-r shathe mel khay, noi arekjon er golpo-r shathe. Ei drishtibhongi manushder jonno je guli agency chan, tar poth nijeri bujhen chan, ebong artho ke odhine nite rajji na.

Shottota:

Absurdism: Shottir Chap

Absurdism birodh theke shuru hoy. Manusher artho chay. Bishsho noshto thake. Manusher khujje khujje kore. Duniya kono jayga dey na. Absurdism ei chap fix korte chay na. Seta shikar kore. Jibon ke bishsho arthor dorkar na banchte hobe. Manusher sthaniyo artho toiri korte pare jokhon bishsho artho nei. Shottota kalponiker beshi bhalo.

Absurdism bhoyabahata encourage kore na. Eta sposhthota encourage kore. Eta manusher ke encourage kore je kokhono ashbe na aise byakhya er pechone lagte bondho koro. Eta manusher ke encourage kore purno banchte jokhon bishsho nijei bujhte rajji na. Absurdism onoshwari noy. Eta nobojonno. Eta manusher ke jibon enjoy korte deye jokhon setar gupto script thakar daabi kore na. Eta manusher ke birodher upor hashite deye noi kade. Eta manushke artho toiri korte deye je proshno korle noshto hoy na. Ei drishtibhongi manushder jonno je guli shottota chan, shokti chan, ebong comfort er beshi toettho chan.

Shongjog:

Gothonamulluk Prokriya ebong Nihilism er Bhumika

Paramparar modhye, dhormo guli byabashayin vabe porishkar, sangskritik, ebong byabashayin vabe bhinno. Kintu tader gothonamulluk prokriya ekta dhara dekhe. Tara arthoke prodanotar upor bindu kore. Tara udheshoke bishsho prodhanotar upor bindu kore. Tara toetthoke prodhanotar upor bindu kore. Tara noitikotake divya gothonotar upor bindu kore. Tara identityke abritto golpor upor bindu kore.

Nostik jibondritter drishtibhongi ei dhara ulte diye. Tara arthoke manusher byabachanar upor bindu kore. Tara udheshoke anubhober upor bindu kore. Tara toetthoke prodhan pramaner upor bindu kore. Tara noitikotake foliter upor bindu kore. Tara identityke sakriyo toiri kormokolaper upor bindu kore. Ei birodh superioriter na. Eta gothonotar. Dhormiyon dritti baire theke bhitore build kore. Nostik dritti bhitore theke baire build kore. Ekta bishsho prodhan theke shuru hoy manusher jiboner dike. Arekta manusher jibon theke shuru hoy ebong bishsho prodhan dhorte rajji na.

Nihilism tin-ti jibondritter pichone boshhe thake. Eta manusher ke mone korenoi karan bishsho artho distribute kore na, udhesho reality-r modhye toiri noy, ebong nishchito bhab prochur vabe abritto. Reflectionism abritto golpo chariye dekhe jokhon nihilism-er dike jay. Existentialism abritto golpo chariye dekhe jokhon artho pre-installed noy bujhe. Absurdism abritto golpo chariye dekhe jokhon bishshor noshto shikar kore. Ei kono drishtibhongi nihilism-er upor pore na, kintu sobar modhye diye jay. Nihilism shudho tai toiri kore jate tin-ti jibondritter drishtibhongi ekta shottito kichhu build korte pare.

Nostikota dritti noy. Eta shima. Eta jaboriyo kalponik, adhutokari prodhanoto, ebong abritto script chariye diye. Jokhon ei guli shoriye gelo, manusher kache banchte ekta poth dorkar je abritto guarantee-r upor depend kore na. Reflectionism, Existentialism, ebong Absurdism ei shimar theke swabhabik vabe grow kore karon tara byabostha chai jekhane artho distribute hoy na, kalponik ichchhamoto, ebong prodhanoto nijer jayga earn korte hobe. Nostikota shudho kore dey. Reflectionism bhitti toiri kore. Existentialism gothona toiri kore. Absurdism gothonake onushthito rakhe. Ei sob milke kalponik chara jibon kharap korte ekta purno gothona toiri kore. Eta manusher ke kalponike enjoy korte deye tar upor rajjito chara, myth chara artho toiri korte deye, ebong shokto hoye na boshsho hoye na. Ei jibondritter drishtibhongi chetona, shahosh, ebong ichcha chai jekhane bishsho answer distribute kore na, kintu shombhobota ache.

Uposhonghar:

Ekta Shobdo Unnayan

Ei tin-ti gothona noshto bishwer modhye diye berote alhada alhada tactics deye. Reflectionism tomar mental perimeter shohito onushthito rakhe. Existentialism bhari tomader kandhar upor diye dey. Absurdism tomar komor shokto kore. Tara shohoj ride ba shohoj pothomaner promise kore na. Tara tomake ekta shafol shuru, ekta dharadhari tool, ebong output-er total ownership deye. Pre-written plan chara duniyate, golpo ta tomader.
