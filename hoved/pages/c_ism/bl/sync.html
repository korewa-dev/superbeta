Songgothito bisshasher dorshon: Shomporko, Dhormo O Puran

Jotil Bishoy

Paabitra Golper Jibon Chakro

Shob manusher bishwas, bharosa, asha, bhoy, ar kolpona korte parar shokti ache. Eta shadharonno monojogin upokoron. Kintu kokhono kokhono bishwas eto shokto hoy je eta pehichan ghotay. Kokhono kokhono bharosa bishwash hoye jay. Kolpona doctrine hoye jaay. Tokhon, bishwas private onubhob hoye roye na, ekta faith hoye jay. Ar jokhon faith followers, rules, golper shathe jure ar gathan niye assemble kore, tokhon eta organized hoye jay.

Jokhon faith byaktir theke boro kichhu hoye organized hoy, tokhon eta tend kore tinta ruper moddhe ekta te crystal hoye jaay: cult, religion, ba pore mythology. Eta bhinno bhinno bishwaser jati na. Eta ek i prokriyar bhinno porjay.

Organized faith ekta golper diye shuru hoy. Kokhono golpo choto hoy, koyekjon manush ekta nirdeshok ke follow kore ta dhore rakhe. Kokhono eta proshongso prothishthan hoye vuray, jekhan mandir, grontho, ar riti ache. Kokhono eta moye jay, pichone shudhu koyek tukro roye je guli biggyanira myth bole. Maap bodle jay, gathan bodle jay, kintu gutiyo uro naki ek i royeche. Manush explaination, sharaam, pehichan, ar byabostha chaay. Tara ekta golpo chaay je duniya ke kom gondogor ar beshi ortho-shali kore.

Shrenibibhag

Cult, Religion, ar Myth

Cult: Organized Faith er Shuru. Cult organized faith er shobchoto rup. Eta prae ekti charisma bhori byaktir diye shuru hoy je bishesh gyaner dabi kore. Goshthi sei byaktir chaardike toiri hoy, rituals, rules, ar golpo guli groy kore loyaltyta dridho kore. Darshon bole kevabe sei authority justify kora hoy. Keno manush swadhinota tere diye? Keno gujho ekta gun hoye jay? Keno birodhi ekta bhoy hote hoy? Keno bishwas proof er cheye shomajik chap diye ghotito hoy? Cult bhoyor pashe thrive kore. Tara intensity er upor nirvor kore. Tara pehichan toiri kore isolaton ar certainty diye. Tara shokti peye goshti jekhan bishesh kichhu jan je puro duniya dekhte chaay na.

Religion: Faith er Bistariton. Religion ekta cult je boro hoyeche. Tar beshi followers ache, beshi gathan ache, beshi itihas ache, ar beshi protishthanik weight ache. Eta golpo, ritual, goshti, ar authority ke miliye diye. Eta naitik framework, samajik ekjot, ar atonmo somporker proshner uttor diye. Darshon ei sob guli ghoroner bhabe porikkha kore. Dhormik dabi guli coherent kina ta jiggash kore. Devotar gun guli ki sense kore ta bhebe dekhe. Doctrine nijer sathe birodhi kina ta porikkha kore. Dhormik bhasha ki kore kaj kore eta study kore. Manush jokhon bhoktike dayalu, shoktishali, ba chirokalin bole, tokhon tara ki bolte chaay ta jiggash kore. Religion tikke thake karon eta boro hoye jay. Eta aisa toiri kore je nirmata thekeo beshi din thake. Eta experiment er cheye riti hoye jay. Eta byaktigoto bishwas er cheye shongskritik smriti hoye jay.

Myth: Faith er Fossil. Myth ekta religion ba cult je ar kono bhoktar nai. Eta ekta mrito faith. Tar devota sahitye chole jay. Tar rituals lokkhon hoye jay. Tar naitik shikkha guli golpo hisebe thake, adesh hisebe na. Darshon myth ke study kore karon tara dekhaay kibhabe manusher kolpona kaj kore. Tara dekhaay samaj kibhabe prokriti, naitikta, ar pehichan ke explain kore. Tara dekhaay bishwas goshti kibhabe vikas pash, bhenge jaay, ar poriborton hoy. Myth ekta porajoy na. Eta ekta fossil. Eta ekti worldview er aakar preserve kore, onek din por o je worldview nijei noshto hoye geche. Myth dekhaay manush kokhon bhoy peyeche, bhalobesheche, ba pujito koreche, even jokhon mul ortho moye geche.

Gothon

Shohoju Gathan ar Khotir Proshno

Organized faith er ei tinta rup eki building block share kore. Tara nirvor kore golpo, authority, ritual, ar goshti er upor. Golpota goshtike ekta shohoju utpatti ar shesh diye. Authority decide kore golpota ki bujhay. Ritual golpota bar bar kore jaay ta pehichan hoye jaay. Goshti golpota jorito kore belong korar ar chap diye. Sob miliye, ei sob guli bishwas ke gathan hoye. Tara shima, dayitto, ar asha toiri kore. Tara bhitorir manush ar bairir manushke alada kore. Tara loyalty ke reward kore ar birodh ke shasti diye. Tara faith ke byaktigoto onubhob theke ekti collective worldview hoye poriborton kore.

Darshon bole ei sob guli ki kore kaj kore ar kibhabe tara manusher bhabna ke prabhavit kore. Keno manush golper upor bharosa kore ta jiggash kore. Keno tara nirdeshoker kotha mone ta bhebe dekhe. Keno tara ritual bar bar kore ta porikkha kore. Keno tara proof na kora bishwas ke defend kore ta jiggash kore. Keno pehichan bishwas er sathe eto ghaniro bhabe jure je question kora ghoti-bodh hoy ta bhebe dekhe.

Ekta mul proshno ache khotir proshno. Jodi ekta devota bhalo ar shoktishali hoy, tokhon keno kosto ache. Koyek riti bole kosto manushke shikkha diye. Anerok bole swadhin ichha risk darkar. Koyek bole khoti bhollar jonno proyojon. Critic bole ei sab explanation khub shotto kosto er shathe milte pare na. Khotir proshno bhoktike bojhate badha diye je ekta bhalo srishtikarta toiri koreche duniyate eto byatha ache keno.

Somproti

Atheism ar Organized Faith

Ekta atheist bolbe je sob bishwas fantasy. Ekta dhormi manush o i bolbe, kintu nijer bishwas bad diye. Ei somproti organized bishwas er bishaye important kichhu dekhaay: protita worldview nijer golp ke sotti mone ar onner golp ke bolego mone. Parthokya holo atheism nijei idea ke reject kore na. Eta fantasy dabi, prokritik bishwas er explanation, ar verify na kora metaphysics ke reject kore. Dhormo prokhap ei sob guli reject kore, kintu shudhu jokhon ei guli protirodhi riti er hoy.

Organized faith goshti ke ek kore, shilpe inspire, ar naitik nostrim dite pare. Eta boner jonnoo justification dite pare, birodh suppress kore, ar ekroopota enforce kore. Darshon bole ei sob effect kibhabe utponno hoy. Keno koyek bishwas shohodarja encourage kore ar onner goshti vibhajan kore ta jiggash kore. Kibhabe protishthan myth ar ritual diye shokti dhore rakhe ta porikkha kore. Keno manush nijeder khoti kore doctrine ke defend kore ta jiggash kore. Keno goshti mul purpose noshto hoye geleo riti ke dhore rakhe ta porikkha kore. Organized faith pehichan o ghotay. Eta manushke bolay tara ke, kothay, ar ki value korte hobe. Eta toiri kore nayok, khuni, saint, paapi, bhitorir manush, ar bairir manushke. Eta duniyate certainty diye je prae dekhaay na. Ei certainty sharaam diye, kintu eta shokto o hote pare. Jokhon bishwas pehichan hoye, tokhon question kora ghotibodh hoy.

Organized faith prae nijer reject kora bishay diye nijer poribokhha kore. Ekta cult onno cult ke reject kore. Ekta religion onno religion ke reject kore. Ekta myth kichu o reject kore na karon tar ar kono bhoktar nai. Ei pattern kokhono atheism er ekta dhoroner sathe miliye jaay. Atheism jeta lack of belief na, eta pehichan hisebe atheism jekhon sob prokritik dabi ke ottoi reject kore jekhon ekta bishwas protirodhi doctrine ke reject kore. Koyek organized faith insist kore je tar golp shudhu sotti golp. Tara onno explanation ke bhoy roop mone. Tara shima toiri kore doctrine ke rokkha kore ar birodh ke shasti diye.

Sonnash

Lokshahajik Bikalpo ar Keno Eta Guronno

Ei khetra lokshahajik bikalpo o explore kore. Koyek chintabid bole naitikta devotar adesh darkar na. Onnerok bole manob somporko, srijon shilpo, ar dayitto diye ortho toiri kora jay. Koyek propose kore je obogun ar chomok prokriti, biggan, ar kala theke ashte pare, prokritik bishwas theke na. Ei abosthan dhormoke akromon kore na. Eta duniya ke bujhar onno onno poddhoti diye. Eta dekhaay je myth er upor ortho nirvor kore na, ar naitikta devotar authority chharao thakte pare.

Organized faith ajkeo manob jibon er shobchoto shoktir moddhe ekta. Eta ain, shongskriti, pehichan, ar ghotona ke ghotay. Eta udarota ar nishthurta inspire kore, ekjot ar vibhajan kore. Eta uddhar korte pare ba manipulate korte. Eta mukti dite pare ba rokka korte. Darshon manushke ei sob jatilta bujhar sujog diye confusion na hoy.

Seshe, organized faith porikkha kora manushke bolena ki bishwas korte hobe. Eta tader diye bishwas ke bujhar upokoron diye. Eta dekhaay kibhabe cult, religion, ar myth kaj kore, kibhabe tara bhabna ke prabhavit kore, ar kibhabe tara samaj ke ghotay. Eta o dekhaay kibhabe koyek dhoroner atheism sei i pattern ke reproduce kore jokhon pehichan inquiry ke replace kore. Eta shothikota encourage kore jokhon emotional bishay dabi sathe deal kore. Ar eta manushke mon kore je faith niye bhebe dekha kono attack na. Eta manob shrishtir shobchoto shokti ke bujhar ekta cheshta.
