Nastik / Nastikota

Otiprakritto kortritto shoriye diye, nastikota manob mullo o sokiyo darsnik kathamo nirmaner jonno ekti dharonaMulok sthong shoriye.

Nastikota Bisshash Mota Mot
Dharona:

Jayga Mochon

Ei prothom bindu-ta guruttopurno karon nostikota kono sakar mul doktriner upor bhitti kore toiri hoy nai. Seta defines je seta ki dabi kore na. Seta devta, adhutokari tothyo, ebong divya prodhanoto ke pratyakhare kore.

Ei negative definition nostikota-ke ekta bhinno fleksibiliti deye. Eta onek noitik, rajnoitik, ebong darsik obostha-r sathe somporko rakhte pare. Eta Antitheism, Secular Humanism, ebong Legal Reclassification byabosthar bhitti hishebe kaj korte pare.

  • Byobashayi nostikota: Manushder byakhya kore je tader shudhu bishwas nei. Tara kokhono dhormiyo bhabna grohon kore nai, ba disbelieve ke sadharon mone kore. Onek manush ei obostha shanto bhabe ebong sakriyo chintar charai pohanchhe.
  • Nyamabaddho nostikota: Disbelief-ke shobcheye nyayosiddho obostha hishebe bole. Ei dharana darsik gaveshona, bigganer tolosho, ba dhormiyo dabi-r gahan porikkha theke grow kore, jekhane prodhan tothyer proman chara prodhan tothyo byabostha proshno kore.
  • Poddhotik nostikota: Bigganer byabohar-e dekha jay. Bigganshok-sra devta ke sidhdhanto kore na; tara shudhu adhutokari byakhya avoid kore karon seta porikkha kora jabed na. Ei vababei naturalism, empiricism, ebong scientific skepticism-er ekta guruttopurno bhitti toiri hoy.

Nostikota orthonitir, noitikotar, ebong porichoyer upor orthonitir boro proshner sathe somporko rakhe. Karon eta kono noitik noiti ba dritti prescribe kore na, nostikota manushderke nijer gothona toiri korte jayga rakhe.

Onek manush Humanism adopt kore. Aranjon Existentialism ba Reductionism-er dike jay. Konek manush saman odhikar-er dike khujje. Aranjon sangskritik toloshor dike. Nostikota ei byabachana guli dictate kore na. Seta shudhu dhormiyo prodhanoto-ke guide korte hobe bole dharata mukto kore.

Ei fleksibiliti bojhai keno nostikota onek alhada obosthar bhitti hishebe kaj korte pare. Antitheism dhormiyo shomajik prabhab-ke challenge kore. Secular Humanism manusher dorkar-er upor bhitti noitik byabostha build kore. Legal Reclassification nostikota odhikar redesign kore jekhane sob bishwas byabostha soman obostha pabe. Pratiti alhada obostha ei prothom bindu theke uthe: devtar abhab.

Nostikota kono dritti noy je manushder ki mone korte hobe. Eta darsik jaygar mochon. Jokhon adhutokari prodhanoto shoriye jay, manushderke nijer gothona build korte pare, toiribidder, prodhan pramaner, ebong manusher obhigrahan-er sahajjo diye. Tai nostikota erokom boro range-r obostha support kore. Eta kono doktrin noy. Eta bhitti.

Mull:

Mull Aktib nostik ba Legal Reclassification Nostik

Legal Antitheist ba Legal Reclassification Nostik

Ekta legal reclassification nostik odhikar define kora kshetre kaj kore. Seta dekhe je dhormiyo ekta surakkhito shrenir modhye ache ebong baki bishwas byabostha baire. Tar udhesho dhormiyo swatantrata shoriye fele na. Tar udhesho setake biroddho kore. Tara ekta soman shreni chai je sob dritti cover kore. Dhormiyo swatantrata ekta boro odhikarer angsho hoy. Kalponik swatantrata. Kalponik theke muktir swatantrata. Bishwas-er swatantrata. Naam bodle jete pare. Gothona roye.

Ei obostha duti obostha-r upor bhitti kore. Prothom: soman odhikar. Pratiti dritti soman ainor surakkha pabe. Ekjon dhormiyo bishwashi ar ekjon kalponik duniya ba kichhu-i na mone manusher moto adhikar pabe. Ditiyo: prodhanotar pratyakhare. Dhormiyo special exemption pete na karon tar beshi bishwashi ache ba lombo itihas ache. Jodi bishwas byabostha byabohar shillir dike prabhav fellay, tahole setake kalponik ba dritti-er moto obostha diye dekte hobe.

Ei duti obostha milke legal reclassification nostik toiri hoy. Tara prodhanotar tolosho chara thame na. Tara odhikar-er gothona redesign kore. Odhikar byakti-ke attach hoy, bishwas label-ke na. Jokhon ain dhormiyo bishwas ke baki kalponiker theke alhada korte bondho kore, shreni muche jay. Kono dritti arekta-r upor noy. Kono dritti arekta-r noyche noy. Ei vababei dhormiyo byabostha ke shobcheye protect kore. Eta legal reclassification nostik chai odhikar-er gothonar modhye shottota, patch noy.

Soman Odhikar Nostik

Soman odhikar nostik samanta-r dike kaje. Seta bole dhormiyo ebong adhunik dritti soman ainor ebong samajik obosthay kaj korte hobe. Ei obostha dhormiye biruddhe noy. Eta obostha abomanito biruddhe. Jodi ekjon dhormiyo byakti tar bishwas-er surakkha pete, tahole ekjon adhunik byakti tar disbelief-er soman surakkha pete hobe. Jodi dhormiyo goshti accommodation pete, tahole somprodaiyoto goshti soman obostha pabe. Ei bhab-ta shikkha, chikitsa, ebong rajnoitik nitir alochana-e uthe. Soman odhikar nostik guli dhormiyo noitik siddhanton special consideration-er biruddhe jaite. Tara bole noitik siddhanton kono bishwas byabosthar upor sacred label ache ba nei tar upor depend kore na. Ekhane ki guruttopurno seta consistency. Jodi samaj swatantrata mull kore, tahole setake soman vabe apply korte hobe. Soman odhikar nostikota secular constitutional niti-r sathe mel khay. Eta byabostha support kore je jekhane byakti nijer dritti nirbashon bhoy chara choose korte pare. Soman odhikar nostik dhormiye kom korte chaibe na. Seta noishchoy kore je kono dritti preferential obostha pabe na.

Prodhanoto-birodhi Nostik

Prodhanoto-birodhi nostik soikhito odhikar guli porikkha kore je dhormiyo rajjo jibone pete. Ei odhikar desh anusaar bodle, kintu prai tax exemption, special accommodation, ebong sangskritik somman antorbhukto. Prodhanoto-birodhi nostik bole ei subidha guli nyayoto nay. Eta ekti obomanito bhougol toiri kore je jekhane dhormiyo protishthan surakkha pabe je baki bishwas byabostha-guli pete na. Ei obostha legal reclassification nostikotar theke shungirno. Eta puro odhikar gothona redesign kore na. Seta nirdishto prodhanotar dike akromon kore. Prodhanoto-birodhi nostik prashna korte pare keno dhormiyo protishthan somprodaiyoto darsik goshtir theke beshi tax benefit pete. Seta dhormiyo noitik siddhantor bishesh consideration dey ain-ke challenge korte pare je dhormiyo motivation general rules theke mukti dey. Prodhanoto-birodhi nostik bole shottota ei subidha muche fele dorkar. Jokhon sob bishwas byabostha soman noiti-r modhye kaj kore, byakti tader dritti instutional chap chara choose korte pare. Prodhanoto muche fele dhormiyo byabostha ke simito kore na. Eta dhormiye automatic uchcho sthan theke mukti dey.

Mitro:

Milroamyo Chinta Byabostha

Ignosticism

Ignosticism ekta challenge diye shuru kore. Kono manushe devtar astitwa niye jukti debar aage, setake porishkar vabe define korte hobe. Ignosticist guli bole God-er byabashayi definition guli onek dhomila ba birodhi-mulok je porikkha kora jabed na. Jodi ekta dharona porishkar vabe byakhya kora na jay, tahole setake arthonitir saath kotha bolte jabed na. Ei obostha devta ke sidhdhanto kore na. Setar porishkar bhasha ke pratyakhare kore. Ignosticism prai dekha jay jokhon manush ek-i shobdo byabohar kore kintu purno alhada bhaba mane. Ekjon byakti ekta byakti devtar chitra tairi kore. Arekjon ekta bishshor shokti mane. Tritiyo ekta pratik mane. Ignosticist guli bole ei definition guli milte na. Alochana shuru hotei puro vabe shoriye jay. Ei vababei nostik toirir sathe mel khay. Jodi God-er dharona porishkar vabe define kora na jay, bishwas ba disbelief guli tobigto hoy. Ignosticist prothome porishkarota chay. Jodi porishkarota eshe na, alochana shesh.

Prakritibad

Prakritibad bishsho ke prakritik karon-er byabostha hishebe mone kore. Je kichhu ache seta sob dhara anujayi. Prakritibad guli protyokkhik gyan-er upor depend kore. Tara adhutokari hastokhep-er dike jay na. Ei obostha gombhir mul ache. Prachin darsok guli may, rog, ebong gotioner jonno prakritik byakhya diyechilo. Adhunik biggan ei vababei bautikbiggan, jibbiggan, roshayan, ebong monobiggan-er dike expand kore. Prakritibad bole na je amra sob bujhte pari. Seta bole je amra je kichhu bujhte pari seta prakritik shoktir chara porikkha korte pari. Prakritibad nostikotar sathe beshi mel khay karon seta divya hastokhep-er dorkar muche fele. Jodi prakritik karon bishsho ke byakhya kore, tahole devtar dorkar nei. Onek nostik prakritibad adopt kore karon seta reality ke porishkar vabe byabashayin kore.

Boutikbad

Boutikbad bole sob kichhu maati diye toiri. Chinta, obhigrahan, ebong chetona shorirer prokriya theke uthe. Ei bhabna onek paramparar modhye dekha jay. Charvaka guli prodhan granth ke pratyakhare kore ebong bole shudhu byoutik duniya-i ache. Greek atomist guli reality ke kanoner kombinotion hishebe byakhya kore. Adhunik boutikbad guli monobiggan ebong bautikbiggan-er upor depend kore. Boutikbad nostikotar sathe bhalo vabe mel khay karon seta immaterial atma ba adhutokari lokkho-r dorkar muche fele. Jodi chetona theke uthe, tahole alhada dhormiyo entiti-r dorkar nei. Boutikbad guli prai proman deye je mon-er obostha r chetona-r poriborton somporkito. Boutikbad byoutik duniya-ke dhoni ebong jotil mone kore. Artho, srijonshilta, ebong obhigrahan maati-r somporker theke uthe. Ei dharana nostik toirir sathe mel khay byoutik duniya-er upor manusher obhigrahan bindu kore.

Byoutikbad

Byoutikbad boutikbad-er adhunik ekta roop. Seta bole je sob kichhu byoutik. Eta byouti, shokti, ebong chetona antorbhukto. Byoutikbad guli bole mon-er obostha moner brain-er byoutik obosthar somporkito. Brain bodle man-o bodle jay. Byoutikbad nostikotar sathe milte karon setar khetre adhutokari tothyo-r jayga nei. Jodi sob phenomena byoutik, tahole devta ebong bhut duniya-r modhye thake na. Byoutikbad guli prai bigganer gaveshona byabohar kore. Brain imaging dekhay kivabe chinta ebong obhigrahan neuron-somuhotir somporkito. Bautikbiggan bole maati ebong shokti kivabe byabohar hoy. Byoutikbad bole na amra chetona purno vabe bujhte pari. Setar bole bole je chetona jekono hoye thakuk, seta byoutik byabostha theke uthe. Eta byoutikbad ke nostikotar ekta shokto mitro kore.

Arthonitir Shongjog:

Byaktigoto ebong Dhormiyo-chara Samadhan

Nihilism

Nihilism ekta kothin poth-e jay. Seta bole jibon-er kono prakritik arthonitir noitikotir ba udheshor nei. Ei obostha bhoyabahito korte pare, kintu etar nostik darsok guli onek prabhavit. Nihilist guli bole noitik byabostha, sangskritik noiti, ebong dhormiyo doktrin manushder toiri. Tara manusher byabashayin byakhya chara byabosthay thake na. Nihilism nostikotar sathe mel khay karon seta divya udhesho-er bhabna pratyakhare kore. Jodi devta nei, tahole bishsho prodhan o nei. Onek manush bhoy diye pratikriya kore nihilism-er upor. Aranjon setake nijer mull toiri kora prothom bindu hishebe mone kore. Jekono obosthay, nihilism bhoomi muche fele dhormiyo arthonitir chara manusher arthonitir shudho ekta bhab roye thake.

Existentialism

Existentialism ekta khokhro byabashayin theke shuru hoy. Bishsho arthonitir distribute kore na. Manusher seta nijer toiri kore. Ei darsota manusher swatantrata, dayitto, ebong bhinno udhesho chara jibon kharap korte challenge-er upor kendro-baddho. Onek existentialist nostik byabosthay kaj kore karon tara divya noishthito kono prodhan praman dekhe na. Sartre bole manusher ekaki boshhe bishsho-jure je kono nirdesho dey na. Beauvoir kivabe swatantrata noitikota ebong somporko ke porinoto kore bichar kore. Existentialism manushder ki mone korte hobe seta dictate kore na. Seta bole bishwas tomader dayitto. Ei dharana nostikotar sathe mel khay karon seta arthonitike manusher kaj hishebe dekhe. Jodi bishshor kono prakritik udhesho nei, tahole devtar dorkar nei. Existentialism manusher ke shoshsho porichoyer saath directly mokhabila korte encourage kore ebong parampara ba prodhanoto theke arthonitir poriborton kore.

Absurdism

Absurdism Existentialism ebong Nihilism-er modhye boshhe. Camus absurd-ke duto toetthor birodh hishebe byakhya kore. Manusher arthonitir khujje. Bishsho seta dey na. Ei chap absurd toiri kore. Absurdism bole na jibon arthohin. Seta bole arthonitir guarantee nei. Camus bole manushder absurd-er pratikriya korte hobe obhijog, sposhthota, ebong shottota diye. Bhoyabahata chara na, purno ebong chetanamoy vabe banchte hobe. Absurdism nostikotar sathe bhalo vabe mel khay karon seta divya udhesho muche fele manusher shoktir dike diye. Seta arthonitir khujjike manusher kaj hishebe mone kore, bishsho prodhan noy.

Reflectionism

Reflectionism arthonitike onushthito rakhe. Setar kono mull, kono bishwas, kono noitik dabi-ke sthan korte hobe earn korte. Ekjon reflectionist arthonitir abritti chara. Seta porikkha kore. Seta porikkha-chara kichhu rakhe na. Eta reflectionism ke byaktigoto arthonitir cognitive backbone kore.

Manabobad

Manabobad noitikota ebong arthonitike manusher agency-er dike kendro-baddho kore. Seta bole manusherke divya prodhanoto chara noitik byabostha toiri korte pare. Manabobadi guli sahanubhuti, sohojogita, ebong byakti ebong samajik goshti-kal-er kollan-er upor kendro-baddho. Ei vababe prachin darsota ebong proyojon abhijan-er theke mul ache. Eta aj-o shikkha, noitikota, ebong samajik nitite byabahar hoy. Manabobad nostikotar sathe mel khay karon seta manushderke nijer noitik byabachanar dayitto mone kore. Seta prodhan granth ba adhutokari adesh-er upor depend kore na. Er boddhe seta toiribiddhi, obhigrahan, ebong sombhok manusher dorkar-er upor depend kore. Manabobad nostikder jonno dhormiyo chara noitik noishthito deye ekta sakar gothona.

Secular Manabobad

Secular manabobad manabobad ekta adhunik nostik obostha. Setar modhye prokar noitikota, biggan, ebong manusher odhikar pramukh niti hishebe ache. Secular manabobadi guli bole noitik byabostha prodhan praman ebong manusher obhigrahan-er upor bhitti hobe, divya prodhanoto-er na. Ei obostha nostikotar sathe kachakachi mel khay. Seta adhutokari dabi ke pratyakhare korar jonno manushderjonno ekta gothonamulluk noitik byabostha deye. Secular manabobad shikkha, chikitsa, ebong rajnoitik nitir alochana-e dekha jay. Seta swatantrata, soman odhikar, ebong gahan chintamoler sahajjo kore.

Utsahi Manabobad

Utsahi manabobad ekta ashabadi poriborton add kore. Setar modhye unnoyon, sahanubhuti, ebong sohojogita guruttopurno. Ei shakha bole manusherke onushthaner madhyome jibon ebong samaj unnoyon korte pare. Seta manusher shokti-r upor kendro-baddho, sima-er na. Utsahi manabobad nostikotar sathe bhalo vabe mel khay karon seta ekta gothonamulluk poth deye. Divya prodhanoto chara, manusher nider bhobishyot toiri korte dayitto nite hobe. Ei dharana sahanubhuti, notun kichhu toiri kora, ebong samajik unnoyone encourage kore. Seta manusher unnoyone prakritik sombhabota mone kore, durdiner alo.

Poddhoti:

Epistemology ebong Songsher Shomuho

Darsik Shongshoy

Darsik shongshoy ekta shohoj abhyas theke shuru hoy. Sob proshno koro. Setar prosna kore je kono toettho ki sambhob, ebong manusherke tar bishwas-er bhittir upor porikkha korte encourage kore. Dhormiyo khetre apply korle, shongshoy dekhay kivabe dhormiyo dabi guli assumptions-er upor depend kore je porikkha kora jabed na. Shongshoy guli prai bole divya prodhanoto prodhan, parampara, ba prodanotar upor depend kore, porikkha kora prodhan praman-er na. Ei parampara nostikotar sathe mel khay karon seta manusherke porikkha-chara bishwas-er biruddhe encourage kore. Jodi prodhan praman eshe na, tahole disbelief nyayoto obostha. Shongshoy nostikota demand kore na, kintu seta nostikotar dike porishkar poth muche fele supports-chara dabi muche fele.

Obhigrahanbad

Obhigrahanbad gyanke obhigrahan-er upor bindu kore. Jodi seta porikkha kora na jabed, porishkar kora na jabed, ba porikkha kora na jabed, tahole setake trust korte paro na. Ei vababe adhutokari byakhya-ke khamata dite pare karon seta sidhdhanto kora na jabed obosthar upor depend kore. Obhigrahanbad guli prakritik duniya-er theke byakhya pasand kore, divya hastokhep-er na. Obhigrahanbad biggan, chikitsa, ebong adhunik darsota-ke porinoto kore. Seta manusherke prodhan praman diye bishwas-er age porikkha korte encourage kore. Eta obhigrahanbad ke nostikotar ekta shokto mitro kore. Jokhon manusher prodanotar chara obhigrahan-er upor depend kore, tahole adhutokari dabi khamata hariye fele.

Tarkabad

Tarkabad bishsho ke bujhar jonno torker pradhan tool hishebe mone kore. Tarkabadi guli bole porishkar chinta, logic analysis, ebong gothonamulluk jukti parampara ba prodhanoto chara toettho dekhte pare. Dhormiyo khetre apply korle, tarkabad birodhi, support-chara assumptions, ebong golpojukto jukti prakasherito kore. Ei parampara nostikotar sathe mel khay karon seta consistency demand kore. Jodi dhormiyo doktrin nijer sathe birodhi ba torker sathe birodhi hoy, tahole tarkabadi setake pratyakhare kore. Tara logik noiti anujayi byakhya pasand kore ebong adhutokari poth chara. Tarkabad nostikota force kore na, kintu seta prai manusherke shetar dike neye jay.

Logical Positivism

Logical positivism ekta kothin noiti toiri kore. Ekta bhabna arthonitipurno hobe jodi seta obhigrahan ba logic diye porikkha kora jabed. Dhormiyo dabi ei porikkha-er modhye jay na. Jodi porikkha kora na jabed, tahole dabi-ta khalifa hoye jay. Seta obhigrahan ba parampara toiri korte pare, kintu toettho noy. Ei obostha nostik toirir sathe bhalo vabe mel khay. Jodi dhormiyo bhabna porikkha kora na jabed, tahole bishwas-er bhitti hishebe kaj korte pare na. Logical positivist guli adhutokari dabi-ke obhigrahan-er abhijog hishebe mone kore, toettho noy. Eta dhormiyo doktrin-ke arthonitipurno alochana-er bahirbhuto kore.

Bigganik Shongshoy

Bigganik shongshoy bigganer poddhoti byabohar kore dabi guli porikkha korte. Setar prodhan praman, porishkar kora, ebong porishkar-porishkarer dorkar. Jokhon keu adhutokari dabi kore, bigganik shongshoy guli porikkha kora prodhan praman khuje. Jodi kono na eshe, tahole dabi noshto hoy. Ei vababei nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta sadharon odhutokari dabi ke porikkha-chara prodhan praman mone kore. Bigganik shongshoy guli abhichalito karon-er sathe biruddhe noy. Tara porikkha kora na jabed dabi ke pratyakhare kore. Dhormiyo prai porikkha-chara dabi-er upor depend kore, tai bigganik shongshoy nostik toirir sathe swabhabik vabe mil kore.

Porichoy:

Adhunik Adhormiyo Gothona

Muktichinta

Muktichinta manusherke prodhan praman-er madhyome bishwas toiri korte encourage kore, noi prodhanoto ba parampara theke. Seta swatantriko chintake ekta moulik daittobolita hishebe mone kore. Muktichinti guli prodanota ba prodhanotar upor depend kora dabi ke prashno kore. Tara byabashayin bhabna pasand kore je porikkha kora, porikkha kora, ba alochana kora jabed. Ei vababei nostikotar sathe mil kore karon seta prodhan granth ba dhormiyo netritto ke toettho dictate korte baddho muche fele. Muktichinta kono doktrin noy. Eta mon-er ekta abhyas je manusherke tar nider dritti-r upor porikkha korte encourage kore.

Brights Andolan

Brights Andolan prakritibadi dritti-er manushder jonno porichoy label deye. Ekjon Bright bishsho ke prakritik noiti-er shasan mone kore ebong adhutokari byakhya pratyakhare kore. Andolan-er modhye prodokkhota ebong bishwas guruttopurno. Seta manusherke tader prakritibadi dharana ke khule bolte encourage kore, samajik chap theke lukiyechilo na. Brights Andolan nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta prakritibad-ke ekta sakar porichoy hishebe mone kore, chup thakar assumptions-er na.

Notun Nostikota

Notun Nostikota ekta adhunik andolan je dhormiye porishkar tolosho-r upor kendro-baddho. Lekhok guli jemon Dawkins, Harris, Hitchens, ebong Dennett bole dhormiyo bhabna guli sob dabi-er moto tolosho diye porikkha korte hobe. Tara dhormiyo ke automatically somman ba tolosho-chara odhikar deyar bhabna ke challenge kore. Notun Nostikota rajnoitik alochana, bigganer chokh, ebong dhormiyo doktrin-er sathe mukhollokhite encourage kore. Seta nostikotar sathe mel khay jekhane disbelief sakriyo vabe ebong janapodhik vabe byakta kora jaite pare.

Post-Theism

Post-theism bole samaj theistic gothona-er dorkar chara bere geche. Seta devtar abhijog noy. Seta sangskritik unnoyon-er dike kendro-baddho. Post-theist guli bole adhunik prashthan, noitikota, ebong gyan byabostha divya prodhanoto-er upor depend kore na. Dhormiyo itihasik artifact hoye geche, jibonter bhitti noy. Ei dharana nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta theistic bishwas-ke manusher purno vabe muchhe fele.

Somprodaiyoto (Rajnoitik Noi)

Somprodaiyoto (rajnoitik noy) dhormiyo prabhab chara jibon-er byakhya deye. Ekjon somprodaiyoto nostik, agnostic, ba dhormiye nostik hote pare. Somprodaiyoto dhormiye biruddhe noy. Seta jayga toiri kore je jekhane manushderke dhormiyo prodhanoto chara nijer mull, abhyas, ebong siddhant toiri korte pare. Ei jiboner vab nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta adhormiyo jibon-ke sadharon hishebe mone kore, boishishto noy.

Byabostha:

Somporkito Obostha

Poribotokbad

Poribotokbad jotil prakritik obostha ke shohoj byoutik prokriya diye byakhya kore. Setar modhye byabostha ke choto-choto angsho-er kombinotion hishebe dekhe. Monobigyan poribotokbad byabohar kore chinta ki neuron theke uthe seta porikkha korte. Bautikbiggan byabohar kore bujhte maati ki vabe kaj kore. Poribotokbad nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta adhutokari byakhya ke byoutik diye muchhe fele. Seta kora thakar dike khujjar encourage kore.

Noityobaddhota

Noityobaddhota bole ghotona purbo ghotona theke uthe. Jodi obostha jante paro, tahole folito bujhte parbe. Ei dharana adhutokari hastokhep-er jayga muchhe fele. Noityobaddhatar guli bole bishsho karon-ebong-fol-er madhyome chole. Ei bhabna nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta divya karjo ke tobigto mone kore. Noityobaddhota jotil-tar pratyakhare kore na. Seta shudhu bole jotil-ta-o prakritik noiti anujayi kaj kore.

Manusher Kendrit Noitikota

Manusher kendrit noitikota manush-er dorkar-er dike noitik byabostha toiri kore, divya adesh-er na. Seta sahanubhuti, kollan, ebong sombhok dayitto-r upor kendro-baddho. Ei vababei manabobad, somprodaiyoto shikkha, ebong adhunik noitik darsotar modhye dekha jay. Eta nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta noitikota-ke devtar theke uthe eshechilo bole muchhe fele. Er boddhe, seta noitikotake manusherke milkore toiri kora kaj hishebe dekhe.

Somprodaiyoto Prakritik Ain

Somprodaiyoto prakritik ain noitikota prakritik manobabashya theke derive kore, divya adesh-er na. Seta bole noitik niti manusher byoboshay, monobigyan, ebong somajik somporkor theke uthe. Ei dharana nostikotar sathe mel kore karon seta noitikotake porikkha kora byabashayin obostha-er upor bindu kore. Somprodaiyoto prakritik ain noitik noiti-ke manusher jiboner angsho hishebe dekhe, adhutokari strot-er nirdesh noy.
