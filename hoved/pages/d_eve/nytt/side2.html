Attacks on Atheists and Secular Activists Worldwide

Reporting on the violence, harassment, and legal persecution faced by atheists and secular writers around the world

Public atheists and secular activists continue to face harassment, imprisonment, and violence in many countries. In several states, simply declaring oneself nonreligious is a criminal offence, and apostasy laws make open criticism of religion dangerous.

The pattern

The attacks follow a familiar pattern: a writer publishes a critique, social media amplifies the outrage, and authorities respond by silencing the critic rather than protecting them. In some countries, bloggers have been killed simply for questioning religious doctrine.

What secular groups are doing

Secular organizations document these cases, offer legal support, and campaign for freedom of expression. Their reports are often the only record of abuses that governments prefer to ignore.

Why it matters

Freedom of belief includes the freedom to reject belief. When atheists are attacked for their views, the right to think independently is under threat everywhere, not just in the countries where the violence occurs.
