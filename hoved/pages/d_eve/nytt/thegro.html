The Growth of Secular Organizations Worldwide

How secular and humanist organizations are growing globally, what they do, and why their numbers are rising.

Secular organizations are growing in number, membership, and influence around the world. Bodies like Humanists International connect national groups on every continent, while many countries have seen new freethought and atheist associations form in recent decades. Their rise tracks the broader growth of the nonreligious population.

What They Do

These organizations provide community and support for the nonreligious, from counseling and ethical ceremonies to school and prison outreach. They advocate for separation of religion and state, defend the rights of atheists and apostates, and campaign against discrimination, including in Muslim-majority countries where leaving religion carries real risk.

Growing Influence

Their work increasingly shapes law and policy. They have helped protect free expression, push for humanist naming ceremonies, and challenge blasphemy and apostasy laws. In Bangladesh, secular groups contribute to public debate and defend critical voices under pressure.

The Future

As the nonreligious population grows, so does the demand for organized representation. Secular organizations are becoming normal, established voices in public life, ensuring that people who live without religion are counted, heard, and protected.
