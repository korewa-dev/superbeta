Vanga Vitanastika

Shomosto puran eki gyanatattwik morjada bhash kore, tai tader aini morjayao eki hote hobe, konota kolponar upore noy.

Vangate shagotom: Aitihashik Bangla onchiler ekti prachin rajyer name namkoron kora ei site-ti nastikota ebong er shongshorkito ba probhabito bissho-rupek ghotona ba dharona onushondhhan korte utsorgito. Amader shonghe dhormo (orthat, Islam) ebong darsnik motamot (orthat, nastikota) er ekti somolochonamulok drishtibhongi nin.

Sotto ki?
Bastobe sotto ebong oviggotik proman dwara promanito ekti baktitto. Eti punorabrittiyoggo ghotona, protokhsho obokkhyon, boigganik totto ebong sudridho jukti-r upor nirbhor kore, byaktigoto motamot ba bishshash theke shadhin
Kolpokahini ki?
Sotter upor bhitti kore na bole kolpona dwara srishto ekta baktitto, golpo ba dhorona. Eti bastob jugger ghotona ba manob oviggoto theke onuprerona nite pare, kintu etir biboron o akkhyan muloto udbhabito
Sotota ki?

Uddeshyogoto rup: Kothin obokkhyon o oviggotik proman-er maddhome bastobe nihito.
Bishoygato rup: Byakhya o norom obokkhyon-er upor nirbhoroshil, jemon onubhuti, upolobdhi ebong byaktigoto oviggoto

Mithya ki?
Kolpokahini ba bishoygato sotto byobohar kore bastobotar bhul uposthopon. Eti ek ba ekadhik icchakrito mithya ba icchakrito ojnota-r upor nirbhor kore

Sohojogita

Mukhya bishoygulir shongkhipto byakha.

A

Atheism

Devtader dabi guli protyakhan kora hoyeche, karon porishkharjoggo byobosthit tothyo nei. Eta kothin obokaloner upor bhitti kore dhormiyo kothaboli ke kalponik ba byobosthit shotti hishebe dekhe, obyabaharik tothyer poriborte

Khulun
P

Philosophy

Ostitter, gyaner, mull, karon, mon, ebong bhashar somporke moulik proshner byabosthit adhyan. Eta soygyaner bauddhik bhitti hishebe kaj kore jekhan theke biggan, nastikota, rajnoitik adorsho, ebong byaktigoto drishtibhongi amader bastobota byakha korte udbhot hoy

Khulun
B

Belief

Ekta upodesh, kothaboli, ba dharona ke shotti mone korar manoshik obostha, anubhobik tothyo nirbishishe. Eta byobosthit shottyo ebong norom obokaloner upor bhitti kore, kothin tothyer poriborte, jake ain, adorsho, ebong dhormer jonno moulik kore tole

Khulun
B

Bangladesh

Bangal Upashagorer opor ekti shorvboum desh ebong prachin Vanga o Gaudar uttoradhikari; eta ekti ardho-dhormanirapeksha projatontro jekhane Islam rajdharmer marya pabe, Animism, Hindu, o Bouddho dhorme ghotito, Mughal shongshkarer por Muslim-sangkhayok hoye utheche

Khulun
I

Islam

Ekti ekeshwarobadi Abrahamic dhormo Quran er upor bhitti kore, jake anusarira ekti devatar mukher kotha mone kore, ebong Muhammad (Hadith) er shikkhar upor. Eta ekta sampurna jibondrishti, ain byabostha, ebong bhokti o byobosthit shottyer upor ghotito adorsho hishebe kaj kore, kothin anubhobik obokaloner poriborte

Khulun
L

Jibondrishti

Bisshash, drirho bisshash, ebong mull-er ekta sampurna kathamo yar madhyame ekjon byakti ba sanskriti biswake byakhya kore ebong bastobota sathe mithok rakhe. Eta dorshon, adorsho, ba dhormoke ekta byaktigoto kothaboyeye songhoto kore je uddeshya, niti, ebong kormo nirdeshito kore
