What is a fact?
A statement that is true in reality and proven by empirical evidence. It relies on repeatable events, direct observation, scientific theory, and solid reasoning, independent of personal opinion or belief
What is fiction?
A statement, story, or concept that is created by the imagination rather than being based on facts. While it may draw inspiration from real-world events or human experiences, its details and narratives are fundamentally invented
What is truth?

Objective Form: Grounded in reality through hard observation and empirical evidence.
Subjective Form: Dependent on interpretation and soft observation, such as feelings, perceptions, and personal experiences

What is Falsehood?
A misrepresentation of reality using fiction or subjective truths. It relies on a or multiple deliberate lies or intentional unawareness
