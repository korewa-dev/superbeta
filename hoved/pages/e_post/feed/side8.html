Space Exploration Milestones

From Sputnik to Mars rovers, how key missions expanded scientific knowledge and reshaped our view of the universe.

Space exploration began with observation and balloon flights and advanced to rockets that carried humans beyond Earth's atmosphere. Each milestone expanded both scientific knowledge and human ambition.

Key Achievements

Sputnik opened the space age in 1957. Twelve astronauts walked on the Moon between 1969 and 1972. Robotic probes have since landed on Mars, orbited Saturn, and studied the outer planets, sending back data that reshapes our understanding of the cosmos. The Voyager spacecraft now travel beyond the solar system.

Science and Daily Life

Space research produced GPS, weather forecasting, and satellite communications that billions of people use daily. Planetary science also informs climate models for Earth.

The Road Ahead

New missions target the Moon and Mars, while private companies lower launch costs. Exploration continues to answer fundamental questions about our origins and our place in the universe.
