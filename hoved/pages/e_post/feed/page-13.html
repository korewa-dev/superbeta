Evolution of Internet

From a military research network to a global public square, the internet's history and the challenges now shaping its future.

The internet grew from a military research network into a global infrastructure that mediates work, learning, and relationships. Its evolution reflects decades of engineering, economics, and social change.

From ARPANET to the Web

ARPANET connected university computers in 1969. The World Wide Web, introduced in 1989, made the network navigable with pages and links. Broadband and smartphones brought the internet to billions of people.

A New Public Square

The internet enables free expression, education, and commerce worldwide, including in Bangladesh, where it supports remote work and digital services. But it also concentrates power in a few platforms and spreads misinformation.

Challenges Ahead

Questions of access, regulation, and trust will shape its future. The internet remains a human creation, and its direction depends on informed citizens and accountable institutions.
