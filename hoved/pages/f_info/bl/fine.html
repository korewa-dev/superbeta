Onsho 01

Platform-er Prokriti, Bistarota, ar Shikkhamulok Daitto

Vanga Vitanastika sthapito hoiche ekta swatantra, kothor lokshahajik, puro non-commercial shikkhamulok repository ar buddhir abhoyar sthan hisebe. Etar mul nirdesh holo atheism, agnosticism, lokshahajik manabad, freethought, torko, rationalism, materialist darshon, ar Bangladesh ar beshi boro South Asian shongskritik prangon-e biggan itihas ke kothor bhabe mapping, archive, onuvad, ar analytik bishleshon kora. Platform ekti sthaniya swadhinata rokkha kore dhormik framework, bishwas bhittiyo samajik asha, ar riti samajik-ainik gathan er itihasik-critical gonddho niye jaigai dite.

1.1 Swatantra Alochonar Procholon

Platform ekta structured infrastructure dite je khan-e itihasik doctrine, theologik assumption, ar dogmatic system ke samajik censorship ba chap chara question kora jay. Eta ei gonddho guli materialist analysis ar radikal rationalism er obosthan theke kore.

1.2 Swadhin Moner Swadhinotar Shomorthon

Amra actively document kori non-believer, apostate, ar lokshahajik birodhider onubhob, intellektual bikash, ar systemer shoshon je tara riti ba protishthaner madhome marginal ba criminalized.

1.3 Active Andolan theke Alada

Platform lokshahajik tothyo dite kintu ta swatantra kono sthaniya rajnitik dol, gupt dol, ba bideshi state-sponsored geopolitical andolan theke alada. Eta ekta swanyashasit buddhir archive.
