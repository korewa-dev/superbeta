title: Climate Change Impacts
description: The observed effects of a warming planet, the particular dangers facing South Asia, and the evidence-based response.
---

Climate change is no longer a distant prediction. Rising temperatures, shifting rainfall, and extreme weather are being observed across the planet, with consequences that fall hardest on the most vulnerable populations.

## Observable Effects

Glaciers and ice sheets are shrinking, sea levels are rising, and heat waves are becoming more frequent and intense. Coral reefs are bleaching, and crop yields in some regions are already declining.

## Regional Realities

In South Asia, including Bangladesh, intensified cyclones, flooding, and salinity in coastal areas threaten homes and farmland. Low-lying nations face some of the highest risks, despite contributing least to global emissions.

## Responding with Evidence

Scientists agree the window for effective action is narrow. Reducing emissions, adapting infrastructure, and protecting ecosystems are supported by data. Clear-eyed, evidence-based policy offers the best path to limiting harm.
