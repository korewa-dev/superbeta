---
title: Ancient Greek Philosophy
description: How Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle built the foundations of science, ethics, and critical thinking through reasoned inquiry.
---

Ancient Greek thinkers asked questions that still shape modern thought: What is knowledge? How should we live? Their answers laid the groundwork for science, ethics, and democratic debate.

## The Pioneers

Socrates insisted on questioning assumptions. Plato developed systematic ideas about reality and justice, while Aristotle studied nature through observation, helping found biology and logic. Later schools such as Stoicism and Epicureanism turned philosophy toward practical ethics for everyday life.

## Methods That Endure

The Greeks introduced reasoning based on evidence and argument rather than authority. This spirit later fueled the scientific revolution and continues to influence how secular societies evaluate claims and beliefs.

## A Lasting Legacy

Greek philosophy showed that human reason, freely exercised, can challenge tradition and superstition. Its commitment to open inquiry remains a cornerstone of critical thinking and humanism today.
