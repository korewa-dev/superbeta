---
title: Blockchain Explained Simply
description: A plain-language explanation of how blockchain works, where it helps, and where it falls short of the hype.
---

Blockchain is a shared digital ledger that records transactions across many computers at once. Because no single party controls it and each block is linked to the previous one, past entries are difficult to change without detection.

## How It Works

When a transaction is verified, it is grouped into a block and added to the chain. Copies spread across the network, so tampering would require altering most copies at the same time, a costly barrier for any attacker.

## Uses and Limitations

Beyond cryptocurrencies, blockchains are tested for supply chains, digital identity, and voting. However, they are slow, energy-intensive, and not always the best tool. For many problems, a simple database works better.

## A Balanced View

Understanding blockchain's trade-offs, rather than treating it as magic, is essential. Evidence and scrutiny, not hype, should guide decisions about where this technology genuinely helps.
