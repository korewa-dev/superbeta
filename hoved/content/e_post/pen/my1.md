---
title: My Journey From Faith to Evidence
description: A first-person account of trading inherited belief for the harder, truer work of following evidence.
---

For most of my life, I did not choose my beliefs. I inherited them the way I inherited my family name. Then a series of honest questions led me onto a path I never planned: from faith to evidence. It has been the most difficult and rewarding journey of my life.

## The Unraveling

It began when I applied the same skepticism I used on other religions to my own. If other believers were sincere but wrong, why was I different? I started reading — history of the scriptures, comparative religion, science — expecting my faith to be strengthened. Instead, answer by answer, the evidence pointed elsewhere. It was not that I stopped believing in God's existence overnight. It was that I could no longer justify believing in it.

## The Rebuilding

Letting go felt like standing in a room where a wall had been. But slowly I rebuilt, this time with foundations I could inspect: reason, evidence, and a morality I chose myself. The world became more beautiful, not less. I understood finally that certainty was never mine to hold; understanding is.

I do not regret the years of faith. They taught me what belief feels like from the inside, which is why I speak of it gently. But I will never trade evidence for comfort again.
