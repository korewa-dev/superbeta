---
title: The History of Secularism
description: How the separation of religion and state grew out of religious wars, and why it remains the fairest framework for diverse societies.
---

Secularism is not a rejection of religion. It is a settlement between religions. It emerged from centuries of European religious war, when it became clear that no single church could be imposed on everyone without endless bloodshed. The solution was to build public life on shared civic rules rather than contested divine ones.

## From Peace of Westphalia to Modern States

The path ran from grudging toleration to the formal separation of church and state. Philosophers argued that government derives legitimacy from citizens, not from heaven, and that conscience cannot be compelled. Modern constitutions, from the United States to India and Bangladesh, embed these principles.

## A Framework, Not a Faith

Secularism does not say religion is false. It says the state should not pick favourites. In a society of many faiths and none, that neutrality is what protects everyone equally.

Far from being the enemy of religion, secularism is often the only thing keeping any single religion from becoming the oppressor of all the rest.
