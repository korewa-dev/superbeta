---
title: The Evolutionary Origins of Religion
description: Why religion looks less like divine truth and more like a product of natural selection, built from our biases and social instincts.
---

Religion is universal, and for many people universality seems to prove divine origin. But evolution offers a different explanation: religion may be a by-product of mental capacities that evolved for other reasons. It spreads because it works, not because it is true.

## The Ingredients of Faith

We have brains that detect patterns, imagine minds behind events, and form strong attachments to groups. These abilities helped our ancestors survive. But they also produced a hyperactive sense of agency that turns rustling leaves into predators and coincidences into signs. When a society of such brains starts asking why the sun rises, gods are the answer.

## Why Religion Survives

Religious communities offered real advantages: shared rituals, cooperation, and comfort in the face of death. Groups that cohered around belief outcompeted those that did not. Natural selection therefore preserved both the belief and the institutions built on it.

None of this proves gods do not exist. It does show that we need no supernatural explanation for why people believe. The machinery of religion is already inside us.
