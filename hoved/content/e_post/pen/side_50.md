---
title: What I Wish Religious People Knew About Atheists
description: A gentle correction of the most common misunderstandings believers hold about those who do not believe.
---

There are things I wish my religious friends and family understood about me, and about nonbelievers in general. None of them are accusations. They are simply the way it is.

## We Are Not Angry at God

It is strange to be told I am rebelling against a god I do not believe in, or angry with a deity who, from my view, does not exist. You cannot rebel against nothing. Most of us are not bitter; we are just unconvinced.

## We Did Not Choose This

No one wakes up and decides to stop believing because it would be more fun. For most of us it was the opposite. We believed, we asked questions, and the answers failed us. It cost us something. It was not a lifestyle choice; it was an unwelcome conclusion.

## We Still Have Morality

We do not believe morality needs a divine enforcer. We care for others because they matter, not because we are watched. Goodness without a reward is not less real; it may be more real.

We are not a threat. We are your neighbours, and we would like to be understood as easily as we try to understand you.
