---
title: Religious Explanations Before Science
description: Why humans once credited gods for storms, plagues, and eclipses — and what that history reveals.
---

Long before science, humans needed to explain a world that was beautiful and terrifying. Why the thunder? Why the flood, the eclipse, the illness? Without instruments or method, we reached for the most powerful explanation we could imagine: the gods. Religious explanations were not foolish. They were the best tools of their time.

## Filling the Gaps

Every natural event had its divine cause. Storms were anger, plagues were punishment, eclipses were omens. Each explanation gave meaning and, importantly, a course of action — pray, sacrifice, repent. The god-of-the-gaps did real emotional work, converting terror into ritual.

## The Gaps Filled

One by one, science closed those gaps. The weather became atmospheric pressure; plagues became microbes; eclipses became predictable orbits. None of these discoveries required rejecting wonder. They required rejecting premature certainty. The gods did not retreat because science was arrogant. They retreated because each gap, examined honestly, proved to be natural.

The history carries a warning that I take to heart: every remaining mystery is not automatically divine. Lightning was not a god's weapon; it was electricity. I do not claim science explains everything today. I claim it has never stopped trying — and that patient willingness to be wrong is exactly what faith, at its weakest, refuses to be.
