---
title: The Scientific Method vs Faith-Based Reasoning
description: Why science builds self-correcting knowledge while faith protects its claims from testing, and what that difference means.
---

Science and faith are sometimes described as two windows onto the same truth. But the way they handle error could not be more different. Science is built to be wrong in public; faith is built to never be wrong at all. That single difference explains most of the conflict between them.

## The Self-Correcting Engine

The scientific method demands that claims be tested, repeated, and shared. When an experiment fails, the theory must change. Results are published so others can check the work. Every scientist stands on knowledge that could be overturned tomorrow. This is not weakness; it is the source of reliability.

## The Closed Circle

Faith-based reasoning works in reverse. The core claims are protected from testing by definition, and doubt itself is treated as a moral failure. Explanations are found after the fact for why a prayer failed or a prophecy did not come true. The method guarantees the conclusion.

We trust science to fly our planes and treat our diseases. The reason is not that scientists are smarter. It is that their method makes truth the goal, not the preservation of a belief.
