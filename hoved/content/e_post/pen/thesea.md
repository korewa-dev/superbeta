---
title: The Search for Evidence of the Supernatural
description: What the long hunt for ghosts, miracles and other paranormal phenomena has actually turned up, and why the cupboard stays empty.
---

For as long as there have been witnesses, there have been ghosts. People report levitating saints, glowing apparitions, and answered prayers every year. For just as long, investigators have been trying to verify these reports. The gap between the claims and the evidence is one of the most consistent findings in the history of human inquiry.

## An Empty File Cabinet

Hypnosis has been investigated, and found to be suggestion. Ghosts have been chased with cameras, and found to be drafts and camera flare. Miraculous healings have been examined, and found to be natural recovery or misdiagnosis. Not a single case has ever produced evidence that survives scrutiny. The ones that resist explanation are usually the ones with the least documentation.

## A Fair Test

Psychics fail under controlled conditions. Prayer studies show no measurable effect. This does not prove the supernatural cannot exist, but it proves something important: extraordinary reality does not behave as if it were real.

Given two thousand years of looking, an empty file cabinet is itself a finding. We should weigh it accordingly.
