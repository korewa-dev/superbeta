title: Ancient Mythologies and Modern Religions
description: How the myths of vanished civilizations live on inside today's religions, and why seeing the pattern loosens their hold.
---

The pantheon of Egypt, the Greek gods on Olympus, the Mesopotamian creation epics — none of these seem like "real" religion to believers today. Yet each was once defended with the same certainty we now hear for modern faiths. The only difference is that theirs lost.

## The Borrowed Blueprint

Modern religions did not appear in a vacuum. Flood stories, virgin births, dying-and-rising gods, and moral codes all appear in texts written long before the Abrahamic scriptures. Noticing this lineage was the first step in my skepticism.

## What the Pattern Shows

Every era treats its own mythology as revealed truth while dismissing older ones as superstition. That symmetry is hard to unsee once you notice it. The gods people stopped believing in yesterday were exactly as real to them as any god is to us today. History does not give us a way to choose which mythology is true. It gives us something better: the awareness that all of them are human creations.
