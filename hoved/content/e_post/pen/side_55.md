---
title: Why Extraordinary Claims Require Extraordinary Evidence
description: How the burden of proof works, why miracles and gods demand more than testimony, and why the principle is not a bias.
---

If someone tells me they own a dog, I believe them on a word. If someone tells me they saw a unicorn in the garden, I want more. This is not snobbery; it is rational weighing. The more a claim departs from ordinary experience, the stronger the evidence must be before it is reasonable to accept it.

## Why the Standard Scales

Every piece of testimony is fallible. People misremember, exaggerate, and are sometimes sincere and wrong. Against a normal claim, that fallibility is harmless. Against a miracle, a resurrection, a fulfilled prophecy, fallible testimony is not enough, because the claim overturns everything else we know about the world.

## What Counts as Extraordinary Evidence

It would be a documented, repeatable demonstration: a healed amputee observed by doctors, a prophecy made before the event, a supernatural event that could not be confused with coincidence. Such evidence has never been produced. When believers call the standard unfair, they are really asking that their claims be exempt from the scrutiny everything else faces.

Extraordinary claims are not rejected out of stubbornness. They are rejected because they have never been backed by evidence equal to their size.
