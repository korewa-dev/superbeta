---
title: The Development of Creation Myths
description: How creation stories grew from local explanation into written scripture, and what that development reveals about their human origins.
---

Every culture has asked where the world came from, and nearly every culture answered with a story. The details differ, but the pattern is remarkably consistent: a divine being, often lonely or bored, speaks or shapes or fights the world into existence. This consistency says less about revelation than about shared human psychology.

## From Story to Scripture

Creation myths began as oral tales, adapted to the needs of the people telling them. They explained the weather, the seasons, and why life is hard. Only later were they written down, edited, and declared sacred. We can watch this process in the Bible's two different creation accounts sitting side by side in Genesis.

## What They Reveal

Myths carry genuine information, but it is information about their authors. They reveal what ancient people feared, valued, and observed. Read this way, they are fascinating anthropology. Read as literal history, they fall apart.

We do not need to mock these stories to outgrow them. We only need to notice that they were written by people, just like us.
