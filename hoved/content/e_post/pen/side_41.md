---
title: The History of Atheism Before the Modern Era
description: Atheism is not a modern invention. Unbelief existed in ancient Greece, India and the Islamic world, long before secular modernity.
---

It is often assumed that atheism is a recent product of science and secularism. History shows otherwise. Doubt about the gods is nearly as old as belief, appearing in cultures from ancient Greece to India, and even within the Islamic world.

## Ancient Sceptics

The Greek philosopher Theodorus earned the nickname the Atheist, and atomist philosophers described a universe of matter and chance with no need for gods. In India, the Carvaka school rejected the Vedas and the afterlife outright. These were not fringe accidents but organised schools of thought.

## The Medieval Question

In the medieval Muslim world, thinkers wrestled openly with the question of whether God's existence could be established by reason at all. Some concluded that it could not, and that prophecy was a matter of persuasion rather than proof. They were condemned, but their arguments were recorded and studied.

The term atheist may be modern, but the position is ancient. Unbelief has always been one of the ways human beings have answered the question of what exists.
