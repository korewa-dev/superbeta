---
title: The Expansion of Humanist Movements
description: How humanist movements are growing across the world, from Bangladesh to the global stage, and what draws people to a secular worldview.
---

Humanist movements are expanding worldwide as growing numbers of people identify as atheist, agnostic, or nonreligious. National and regional humanist groups now count hundreds of thousands of members, from Europe's established organizations to newer communities in South Asia, Africa, and Latin America.

## Why People Join

Many arrive through disillusionment with organized religion, others through education and exposure to science. Humanist groups offer what many seekers miss: community without creed, ethics without supernatural authority, and celebration of life without dogma.

## Growing Networks

International federations connect these groups, sharing resources on rights, education, and advocacy. In Bangladesh, humanist and freethinker networks work alongside secular activists to defend free expression and rational inquiry, building spaces for questioning in a religious society.

## The Appeal

The movement's growth reflects a simple idea: that people can live meaningful, moral lives through reason and compassion alone. As literacy and internet access rise, humanism's appeal will likely continue to spread across the globe.
